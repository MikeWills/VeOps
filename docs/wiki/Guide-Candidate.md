# What a candidate sees

Candidates never sign in to VE Ops. Everything they touch is an email or a public page. This page
exists so that whoever is answering their questions knows exactly what they were sent.

It is written from the candidate's side. Point them at it if it helps.

## Registering

You register for an exam session in **ExamTools**, not here. VE Ops picks your registration up
shortly afterwards and takes over from there.

## The confirmation email

Within an hour or so of registering you get a confirmation email with:

- The session's date and time, given in **two time zones** (Eastern and Pacific). Sessions are
  remote and the gap between those two is always three hours, so you can work out Central or
  Mountain from either.
- The **join link** for the online session, if the team uses one.
- A **payment link** for the exam fee.
- A second, **youth-rate link** where the team offers it.

If the team offers a youth rate and you qualify, use the youth link rather than the standard one. It
asks you to confirm you are a youth and then sends you to checkout at the youth price. Paying the
standard rate and asking for the difference afterwards is a manual fix for somebody.

Nothing arrived? Check junk first, then tell the team — most often the address is not what they
think it is.

## Paying

Pay before the session. **No fee, no test** — the VEs enforce that at the door, and it is not
something the software will let slide on the night. If reminders keep arriving after you have paid,
tell the team; a payment made from a different email address than the one you registered with does
not match itself up automatically, and somebody has to attach it by hand.

## After you pass

Two things happen, and the second one is yours.

1. **The team files your session with the VEC.** You do not do anything for this.
2. **The FCC charges its own application fee**, separately, through
   [CORES](https://apps.fcc.gov/cores/userLogin.do). This is not the fee you already paid the team.
   ⚠️ **You have ten days.** If the FCC's fee is not paid in that window, your application expires
   and the exam you just passed does not turn into a license.

VE Ops watches the FCC on your behalf and emails you a reminder if that fee is still outstanding. If
the FCC itself is having a bad week, the team can turn those reminders off and tell you directly
what is going on instead.

Once the FCC grants it, your call sign appears in the FCC's own
[license search](https://wireless2.fcc.gov/UlsApp/UlsSearch/searchLicense.jsp).

## Your data

What the app stores, for how long, and what is deleted afterwards is on the deployment's own
**Privacy** page — linked at the bottom of every page of the app.
