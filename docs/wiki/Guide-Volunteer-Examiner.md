# Volunteer Examiner

Most VEs never sign in to VE Ops. You are on your team's roster, the app knows your call sign and
your contact details, and it emails you when there is something to turn up to.

Two things you might do yourself.

## Keeping your own details current

There is a self-service page for exactly this — no account, no password.

1. Go to the VE sign-in page and enter your email address.
2. The app emails you a sign-in link.
3. Follow it, edit your details, done.

Some things worth knowing about that link, because they are deliberate and will otherwise look like
bugs:

- **It works once**, and it is used up the moment you follow it. If you go back to the email and
  click again, it will not work — request a new one.
- **It lasts 30 minutes**, and so does the session it gives you. Close the browser and you are out.
- **You can only ask every five minutes.** Asking again immediately will not send a second email.
- **Every failure says the same thing** — expired, already used, never issued. The page cannot tell
  you which, on purpose: a page that says "no such VE" tells anyone who asks whether a given person
  is on the roster.

Enter your address and nothing arrives? Check junk, then ask your Team Admin whether the address on
your record is the one you just typed.

## Session invitations and reminders

Your team's Session Managers can invite the roster to a session, and the team's message rules can
send reminders and per-session summaries by email, Discord, or both. Whether you get any of that
depends on how your team configured it.

Every VE email carries an **unsubscribe link**, and it works without signing in.

## Tags, and why yours changed on its own

Teams use **tags** to mark VEs — accredited for this, available for that. If your team has turned on
Discord tag sync, mapped tags follow your **Discord roles**: hold the role, hold the tag.

So a tag can appear or disappear without anyone touching VE Ops, and a tag changed by hand inside VE
Ops comes back on the next sync. **The place to make a tag change stick is Discord.** Tags grant no
access to anything — they are labels, not permissions.

## If you also have an account

Some VEs are also Team Leads, Session Managers or Team Admins. That is a completely separate login,
and the sign-in link above will not get you into it. See
[Roles and permissions](Roles-and-Permissions.md).
