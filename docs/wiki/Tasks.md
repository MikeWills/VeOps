# Tasks

How-to pages, written once and linked from every role guide whose role is allowed to follow them.
The [role guides](README.md) say *what you do and in what order*; these say *how*.

> **This index is the plan, not the finished set.** Pages marked *planned* do not exist yet — the
> role guides currently carry that ground themselves, and the runbooks in the repository cover the
> "something is broken" half. Pages get written when somebody needs one, not up front; see
> [About this wiki](About-this-wiki.md) for why.
