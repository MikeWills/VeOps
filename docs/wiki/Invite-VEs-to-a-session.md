# Invite VEs to a session

Send one email to some or all of your team's VEs about **one session** — an invitation to work it,
or a reminder a few days out with the date, the Zoom link and how many candidates are registered.

**Who can:** Session Manager, Team Lead, Team Admin, System Admin (see
[Roles and Permissions](Roles-and-Permissions.md)).

## Steps

1. Open the session and press **Invite VEs**.
2. If your team has saved a message for this screen, pick it from **Start from** — the subject and
   message fill in. Otherwise write your own, or edit the starter text.
3. Choose the people. Filter by name, call sign or tag; **Select all shown** takes the filtered list.
   Anyone already on the session's roster, without an email address, or unsubscribed is marked.
4. Press **Send**. The email goes from your team's own address, one copy per person.

## What the placeholders become

| Placeholder | Renders as |
|---|---|
| `{{VeName}}`, `{{CallSign}}` | The person being written to |
| `{{SessionTitle}}` | The session's name |
| `{{SessionDate}}` | Start time in Eastern and Pacific, e.g. *Tuesday, September 15, 2026 at 7:00 PM ET / 4:00 PM PT* |
| `{{ZoomJoinUrl}}` | The session's Zoom link — blank if scheduling has not created one yet (the screen warns) |
| `{{RegisteredCount}}` | How many candidates are registered on the session right now |
| `{{TeamName}}` | Your team |

## Saving a message for this screen

A Team Admin writes it under **Messages**, on the moment **When you invite VEs to a session**. Only
messages on that moment appear in **Start from** here.

> **Not the same as "When you email VEs by hand."** That moment belongs to the VE Directory's
> **Email VEs** screen, which is not about a session — a message written there cannot use the
> session placeholders above, and a message written here is not offered there. Pick the moment by
> which button you will press.
