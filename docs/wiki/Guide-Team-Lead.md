# Team Lead

You can see everything your team's Session Managers can see, and change almost none of it. That is
deliberate — the read-only default is what makes it safe to hand out the role widely.

## What you can do

- **Look at anything session-shaped.** The Sessions list, any session's detail, any candidate's
  detail, Applicant Status, the VEC submission screen.
- **The Renewal Monitor**, which is open to every signed-in user. Checking whether a club member's
  license is lapsing is not a privileged act.
- **The two day-of session actions**, on a session's own page:
  - **Refresh candidates**
  - **Create retest payment**

Those two exist because they are a key part of actually running a session and the Session Manager
might not be available to do them for you.

⚠️ **Refresh candidates is not just a read.** It pulls ExamTools right now, and for anyone newly
registered it also creates their payment link and sends their registration confirmation email. That
is exactly what you want for a walk-in. It is not a "reload the page" button.

## What you cannot do

Everything else: emailing candidates, matching an unmatched payment, editing the VE roster, changing
any setting, filing to the VEC. If one of those needs doing, it goes to a
[Session Manager](Guide-Session-Manager.md) or a [Team Admin](Guide-Team-Admin.md).

Your account records an **assigned manager**. That is a note about who you report to. It grants
nothing and takes nothing away.
